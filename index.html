AI can be useful—and confusing

AI is increasingly part of everyday life, from online banking and customer service to search, messaging and voice assistants. It can also produce inaccurate information or be used by criminals to make scams more convincing.

SafeAI Together provides calm, practical guidance so people can use AI more confidently, protect personal information and recognise suspicious requests.

The pause-and-check rule

If a message creates urgency, fear or secrecy—or asks for money, a password, a PIN or a code—pause. Do not click or reply. Contact the person or organisation using a number or website you already trust.

Unexpected contact?

Hang up or close the message. A genuine organisation should not object to you checking independently.

Voice or video?

Do not treat a familiar voice or face as proof. Use a pre-agreed safe phrase or call back on a known number.

Already paid?

Contact your bank immediately. Use the number on the back of your card or another official source.
